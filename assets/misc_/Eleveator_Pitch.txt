FORGE is a web-hosted observatory for the question every trained model hides: is its confidence earned [1], or has it simply run out of data? [2] Load a working model, flip it from a single point estimate to a Bayesian posterior [3], and see and steer that uncertainty in real time — last-layer Laplace for instant feedback [4], HMC as the gold-standard reference [5]. One interface, two needs: explore whether a model has reached its epistemic limit [2], and visualize and tune that uncertainty with Bayesian tools — with no pipeline to build.
