Final Write-up

The FORGE final report. Rather than standing apart from the Statement of Work, this document is the approved proposal, extended: Sections 1–4 are the proposal as approved, Sections 5–8 are new, Section 9 is the reference list, the proposal's ethics / approvals / appendix tail is renumbered to 10–12 behind the new material, and Section 13 is a glossary. New sections are flagged in amber, so a reader who has already seen the proposal knows exactly where to start — Section 5.

The report is authored in LaTeX (final_report.tex + forge-report.sty) and rebuilt to PDF on every push, on the same CI job that builds the proposal. The graded body — Sections 1–8, excluding front matter, the 9–13 back matter, and inline citations — runs to 10,941 words; the whole document is ∼18,650 words over 51 pages.
