Final Write-up

The FORGE final report. Rather than standing apart from the Statement of Work, this document is the approved proposal, extended: Sections 1–4 are the proposal as approved and unchanged, Sections 5–9 are new (goal-by-goal verdict, research component, results, discussion, conclusion), and the proposal's ethics / approvals / appendix tail is renumbered to 10–12 behind the new material. New sections carry an amber NEW IN THIS DOCUMENT marker beneath the heading, so a reader who has already seen the proposal knows exactly where to start.

The report is authored in LaTeX (final_report.tex + forge-report.sty) and rebuilt to PDF automatically on every push, on the same CI job that builds the proposal. Body word count — Sections 1–9, excluding title page, revision history, table of contents, and the ethics / approvals / appendix back matter — is 8,498 words.

What is new in this document

  • §5 — Were the goals met? A goal-by-goal verdict table with the caveat stated alongside the verdict rather than after it, including the one number the report most needs read correctly.
  • §6 — Research component. The methodological claim: FORGE's base measure is variance, under the law of total variance — not entropy or mutual information. The four capabilities C1–C4 restated as four operations on one variance budget (split / verify / source / locate), EAR as a field along each domain's resolving axis, and the ATLAS closure work.
  • §7 — Results. The model board with references and baselines, the measured accuracy-versus-epistemic-signal frontier, the head sweep, the ATLAS closure factorial, and cross-model readings.
  • §8 — Discussion and inference. What the measurements support, and four stated limits of the instrument.
  • §9 — Conclusion and future work. Named open items in the order they should be attacked.
