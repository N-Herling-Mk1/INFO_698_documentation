Poster

The FORGE conference sheet — 48 × 36 in landscape, drawn as an engineering sheet rather than a slide: title block, zone ticks, and six numbered panels. It is generated from source (build_poster2.py + forge_lib.py) rather than hand-edited, so every number on it is read from the exported board and the sheet can be re-cut without redrawing.

What is on the sheet

ZonePanelWhat it carries
01The budget The base measure — variance under the law of total variance — and why it replaced entropy / mutual information.
02One dial, three verdicts The signature graphic: a single segmented EAR arc carrying three needles — ATLAS +12.51 dB, genre RRM +3.79 dB, phonon −16.90 dB.
03The board Every exported model with its EAR, reducible share, prior leak, and verdict band.
04The FORGE four C1 splits the budget, C2 verifies the split, C3 sources it, C4 locates it — four views of one variance budget.
05The instrument The deployed navigation deck and the FORGE dashboard.
06What the instrument caught Calibration is not trust (the H7 case), the fast path is optimistic about its own doubt, and the measured accuracy-versus-signal frontier.
