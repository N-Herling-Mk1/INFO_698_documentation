Risk Management

The schedule reserves Week 10 explicitly as a recovery buffer. Because the three reproductions are the load-bearing piece of the write-up, the guaranteed floor is all three running to ground-truth inside the phase-1 architecture by Week 5. If a Design / Implementation milestone slips by one week, the integration smoke test (W8) shifts into W9 and the testing window compresses to W10 only. A larger slip triggers a scope-cut conversation with the advisor; the documented descope order is the MCP server (W8) first, then the depth of the Bayesian / visualization layer (W6–W7) — never the three baseline reproductions (W2–W5) or testing.
