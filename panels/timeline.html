Risk Management

The schedule reserves a three-week risk-management window (Weeks 8–10) as a recovery buffer. Because the three reproductions are the load-bearing piece of the write-up, the guaranteed floor is all three running to ground-truth as functional standalone sites by Week 6. If a Phase 1 build slips, the integration and deployment work (Weeks 7–9) absorbs into the window rather than displacing the write-up. A larger slip triggers a scope-cut conversation with the advisor; the documented descope order is the MCP stretch (Week 10) first, then the depth of the Bayesian-tuning / dashboard refinements (Weeks 7–9) — never the three baseline standalone sites (Weeks 4–6) or the write-up.
