Logo & Branding

The FORGE mark and the visual system that supports it. The mark integrates three primary symbols — the anvil, the central flame, and the split-spectrum Gaussian — within a circular technical frame. Each element carries explicit meaning relative to what the FORGE tool does.

Design rationale

FORGE is a Bayesian inference tool — the project is about exploring posteriors in physics-scale parameter spaces. The mark has to say that, and it has to say “this is the act of building a model,” because forging is the operative verb. The anvil grounds the composition; the flame is the inference itself; the Gaussian is what gets forged — the distribution, etched into the fire, becoming the artifact. Everything else — network, frame, wordmark — is supporting structure around that central act.

The anvil

The grounded base. Inference is not free-floating math — it happens on top of an apparatus: data, priors, hardware, calibration. The anvil is that apparatus. Chosen over a workbench or altar because anvils are struck; they take impact and do not move. A posterior is built by repeated strikes against fixed structure.

The flame

The act of inference. Sampling is hot — every posterior draw is energy spent against the parameter space. Flame is also the project's double sign: creation (forging the distribution) and destruction (burning away the mass that the data does not support). The flame remains present behind the anvil throughout the motion sequence as a reminder that inference is an ongoing process, not a one-shot event.

The split-spectrum Gaussian

The cool half is the prior — cold, broad, uninformed. The warm half is the posterior — heated by data, contracted, glowing. The curve is etched into the fire because the distribution is not separate from the inference; it is the inference, made visible. Reading the curve left to right traces the trajectory the model actually walks: from a wide, ignorant blue into a tight, luminous gold.

The constellation field

The network being ignited by the sparks. Each point is a node, each thin line a message between them — in the motion sequence the field is “talking to itself.” This represents the inference graph: the structure that carries information through the model. Stars in the sky because the parameter space is high-dimensional and dark, and the network is what lights it.

The circular frame

A reticle for finding precision inside randomness, not decoration. The tick marks read as instrument calibration — this is a measurement tool. The closed circle says the system is bounded; whatever happens inside the frame is the whole apparatus, with nothing leaking past the edge.

The wordmark

F·O·R·G·E with dot separators, etched in gold. The dots slow the reader down — the name is meant to be read as a verb, one strike per letter. The serif treatment is deliberate: serif faces carry weight and tradition, which matches the anvil's role in the composition. The gold color matches the flame core; the wordmark is itself a piece of the fire.

Derivative marks

The full mark is reserved for primary brand surfaces (this site, the proposal cover, the final write-up cover, the iShowcase poster). For utility surfaces — browser favicon, app icons, social cards — a simplified flame-only variant is used.

Color palette

The system runs from cold to hot. Navy holds the structural surfaces — banner, side nav, anvil, body text on light backgrounds. Cyan is the cool spectrum: the prior half of the Gaussian, the unheated regime. Everything from amber forward is the warm spectrum, ramped by temperature: amber and gold sit at the outer flame and the wordmark; flame and ember are the saturated middle of the fire; ignite is the flame core; magenta is reserved for the hottest point-sources in the constellation field.
